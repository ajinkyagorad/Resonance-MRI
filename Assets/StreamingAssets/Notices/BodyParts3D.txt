Right hand and distal forearm extracted, transformed, subdivided and voxelized; the displayed bone and skin surfaces are decimated to about a tenth of their triangles. The tissue classes (skin, fat, muscle, tendon, cortical bone, marrow, other soft tissue) are derived segmentations of these surfaces; their relaxation values come from published measurements (see Scanner-model).
