# Listing draft

Title: Resonance MRI
Short description: A physically simulated MRI scanner and an anatomical hand: watch coils, fields and spins make an image.

Description: Place a working MRI scanner in your room using Quest passthrough, or view it in a quiet VR studio. Every field comes from the scanner's own windings and currents, and every arrow is a simulated patch of the hand's tissue. Follow ten narrated steps: the magnet, protons, resonance, the signal, gradients, slice selection, readout, phase encoding, the image and the full 3-D volume. Each sentence points at what it describes as it happens. Turn the knobs for field strength, RF bandwidth, gradient current, slice position or matrix size, and the whole simulation is recomputed. Pause, step back or advance at your own pace. The app is an educational simulation using a reference hand; it does not scan the user's body, control MRI hardware or produce diagnostic images.

Languages: English text and generated English narration.
Input: Quest Touch Plus (A/B/stick/X) and hand pinch. Seated or standing; all items movable.
Publisher-provided fields pending: support contact, privacy-policy URL, pricing, age/content declarations and store artwork. Server renders are review evidence, not headset captures.

Tracking and passthrough are provided by the headset to operate the experience.
